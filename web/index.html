skafinity

find it on GitHub!

endless, deterministic procedural songs — ska, rock, country, metal, punk and pop. The whole song is a link.

copy link takes the song with it · the widget re-derives its palette from this page

seed format tag:n[:genre][:vibe] — leave a part out and it rolls every song · share a copied link, get the same song · export is stereo .wav
embed it in your own page: <skafinity-player> — see the light demo, the dark one, and docs/embedding.md.
