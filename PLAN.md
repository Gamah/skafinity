# PLAN

**Everything here must keep working in s&box.** The engine is shared source, not a port:
`sbox-library/Skafinity/Code/Engine/**` compiles into both the s&box library and the web wasm bundle.
So every row below is bound by the same rules — the engine stays framework-free (`System`,
`System.Collections.Generic`, `System.Text` only; no `Sandbox.*`, no Emscripten-isms), anything
web-specific goes in `wasm/Exports.cs`, and any new `Config` field lands in `Cfg.To`/`Cfg.From`
(+ `Cfg.Size`) or it won't survive the JS boundary. `SkafinityPlayer.cs` and `Code/UI/` are
s&box-only and must still compile and drive the engine after each row.

Seed compatibility is **not** preserved: old `vibe:tag:n` seeds are expected to break, and the
audio a seed renders is expected to change from commit to commit. That is accepted — no
back-compat shim, no golden-audio contract, and a changed render is never a regression. The
only reproducibility that matters is *within* a build (web and s&box agreeing, and a pure
refactor proving it moved nothing).

**The problem this list exists to solve is that the genres still sound alike.** `GenreProfile`
now separates what notes they play (harmony tables, tempo bands, chord rhythm). What is still
shared is *what rhythm each instrument plays* and *what shape a song has* — which is most of
what a genre actually is. The top rows are ranked on that, not on effort.

| Rank | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 90 | Per-genre comp figures | The loudest remaining duplication. `RenderKeysBar` serves rock+country+pop off one `KeysOnsets {0,3,4,7}`, and `RenderRhythmGuitarBar` serves rock+country+punk off a single `country` bool — so three genres at a time play the *same comping rhythm*, whatever the harmony underneath. Country = boom-chick (guitar on the offbeats, piano stabs on 2&4); Pop = pad + 16th arp; Rock = a real 2-bar riff motif, not an every-eighth chug; Punk = downstroke eighths, chord per bar. `_hornMask` is 8 slots reused for the whole song — make it call-and-response (2-bar mask, bar 2 answers bar 1), which is the actual ska convention. Now cheap: the figure is a `GenreProfile` entry. |
| 86 | Per-genre bass pattern libraries | Replace the single `int[8]` picked once per song. Rock/Metal/Punk/Pop tables still literally overlap (`{0,0,0,0,0,0,0,App}` is in all four) and every genre shares one `EmitBass` patch, one register, one `BassBalance`. Per genre: its own cells, register, osc/sustain/cutoff. Country = alternating root/fifth with a walkdown; Ska = walking + legato chord-tone and 1↔5 motion; Pop already has its `ChordBars=1` floor. The `Approach` cell already sits on the root where harmony isn't moving, so what's left is the cells and the voice. |
| 82 | Per-genre drum grooves | `GenreProfile.DrumStyle` picks between five cases in `RenderKickSnareGroove`, but rock, country AND punk all resolve to `2` — they fall through to the same `default` straight backbeat. Country's "train beat" (the constant running snare) does not exist in the engine at all; punk's fast eighth-note ride/snare drive doesn't either. Ska's one-drop and steppers and metal's double-kick are the only genuinely distinct grooves. Needs per-genre groove tables (and hat/ride subdivision) the way harmony now has per-genre tables, not a shared `switch` default. |
| 78 | Per-genre section maps + section energy | `BuildStructure()` is still one hardcoded list for all six genres (intro4/chorus8/verse8/chorus8/verse8/chorus8/end2) — a metal song and a pop song have byte-identical *form*, which is sameyness no amount of note variation reaches. Profile-driven, plus an `Energy` float that voices actually read (thin the comp and drop hats in a verse; add density/octave/crash in a chorus). New section types: Bridge, Solo, Breakdown, Pre-chorus. Section lengths in multiples of 4. `GenreProfile` has the slot for it already. |
| 74 | `Pattern` with its own `LengthTicks` | `Pattern { int LengthTicks; Event[] Events }`, free-running against the bar line rather than indexed by bar position. This is the fix for sameyness *within* a song: every pattern is a 1-bar loop today, so bar 2 is bar 1. Positions are already ticks (`Timing`), and pattern tables are already mapped onto the bar through `EighthsPerBar` — so this replaces that mapping rather than introducing one. One mechanism subsumes: 1 bar = today's behaviour; 4 bars = a 4-bar phrase for free; 12 bars = blues; 3 eighths = hemiola. **This replaces the per-genre phrase-shape table entirely** — every shape came out as "A A A [thing]", so the shape isn't the variable; author the variation into the last bar of a longer pattern instead. |
| 70 | Chord vocabulary beyond the triad | No genre ever plays anything but a plain triad or a bare power chord: `RenderKeysBar` builds `{d, d+2, d+4}`, country's guitar `{d, d+2, d+4, d+7}`, rock/punk/metal `{root, +7, +12}`, the ending `{d, +2, +4, +7}`. Nothing anywhere adds a 7th, 6th, sus, or 9th, which is why every genre's harmony reads as the same primary-colour chord set even with different roots. Per genre: ska/rocksteady leans on 7ths and 9ths, country on 6ths and sus4, pop on add9/sus2, rock on suspensions, metal correctly stays 3rd-less. Belongs in `Harmony` as a voicing table the chordal voices read, keyed off `GenreProfile`. |
| 66 | Per-genre lead grammar | One `RenderLeadPhrase` for everything — same rest chance, leap logic, register (`_rootMidi+24`) and "phrase every 2 bars". Metal = scalar shred runs; Country = pentatonic double-stops and heavy bends; Pop = a *repeated* 2-bar hook (no genre repeats a motif today); Punk = mostly absent, unison with the guitar when present; Ska keeps the horn behaviour — and now actually varies its voice, since the trumpet/sax/organ/trombone pick is live. Align phrase length to the hypermeasure, and misalign deliberately where the section map asks for transitional dissonance. |
| 62 | De-overlap the scale tables | The progression tables are pruned and test-capped; the SCALE tables never were. Ska/Country/Punk/Pop are all major-heavy near-duplicates (major listed three times plus mixolydian), and Rock ∩ Metal share aeolian and phrygian — so two genres can still draw the identical mode under their now-different changes. Prune the same way, extend the engine test's overlap cap to cover scales, and while there replace the weight-by-duplicate-entry trick (major repeated to bias the `Pick`) with a real weighted draw. |
| 58 | Metric dissonance — displacement + grouping | Biamonte (MTO 20.2) systematises these as *formal functions*: initiating (ambiguous openings), cadential (hemiola accelerating into a phrase end), transitional (fragmentary links), plus large-scale loose-verse/tight-chorus contrast. Displacement = a tick offset added at emit time — near-free now that positions are ticks, and it's the off-kilter feel no amount of note-choice variation produces. Grouping dissonance = `LengthTicks` not dividing the bar (row 74 provides it). |
| 54 | Section feel multiplier (half/double time) | Scale the *pattern* rate per section — no tempo change, no resampling. Metal breakdown, pop drop. Cheapest of the tempo-variation ideas and the highest impact; wants the section map (row 78) first so there is a section to hang it on. |
| 50 | Metal/punk bass follows the riff | Wikipedia *Heavy metal bass*: metal bass is either "a low pedal point as a foundation" or "doubling complex riffs and licks along with the lead guitar and/or rhythm guitars". Both modes are *relational* — neither is an independent pattern table, so a table will always sound like an approximation. Needs the render-order refactor: emit the riff first, have the bass read its onsets. Gives punk the unison option for free. |
| 46 | Fills as patterns, 1 beat – 2 bars | `RenderFill(barTick, 6, …)` is always exactly the last beat of the bar, subdivided 3/4/6 (evenly, via `Timing.EvenSpan`). Once a fill is just a `Pattern`, pick length by weighted draw — 1 beat ~55%, 2 beats ~25%, 1 bar ~15%, 2 bars ~5% — with the 1- and 2-bar fills **gated** to section boundaries that earn them (into a final chorus, out of a breakdown), not available at every phrase end. Also allow fills that start on beat 3 and pickup fills into an entry. |
| 42 | Dynamics — accent and per-note velocity | Loudness is a per-patch constant with two ad-hoc exceptions (the guitar's even-eighth `accent`, the kit's ghost-snare rolls). Nothing else varies note to note or bar to bar, which is the flat, mechanical tell that survives every rhythmic fix above. Wants velocity as a first-class value on an emitted note — a per-genre accent pattern (backbeat weight, offbeat weight, downbeat weight) times the section `Energy` from row 78 — rather than each voice inventing its own scaling. |
| 38 | Anomalous measures / partial-bar links | Biamonte's "anomalous measures", typically 2/4 bars inside a 4/4 context. `Part` carries per-bar tick lengths instead of a bar count; buffer sizing already sums per-part and every render is anchored on `barStart`, so this is tractable. Also fixes the accidental case: the 2-bar `Ending` currently breaks the 4-bar norm at exactly the point a clean landing is wanted — make it 4 and put the irregularity where it does work. |
| 34 | True 2:1 shuffle as a distinct feel | `GenreProfile` draws swing per song from a per-genre band (ska 0.10–0.22, country 0.04–0.16, rock/pop/punk/metal ~straight). What the band can't express is a genuine **2:1 triplet shuffle** (~0.33) — that's a different feel, not a wider range, so widening ska's band to reach it would just make ordinary ska songs sloppy. Wants a per-genre *chance* of drawing a shuffle feel instead (ska/country only) — a second band on `GenreProfile` beside the swing one. `Timing.EvenSpan` already keeps tuplets out of the swing warp, which is the piece this needs, since a shuffle IS a triplet feel and must not be warped twice. |
| 30 | Per-section key (modulation) | `_rootMidi` per section — gives the final-chorus lift. Trivial once sections carry state (row 78). |
| 26 | Per-genre mix profile | `*Balance`, master reverb and width as profile trims: metal dry and mid-scooped, pop wide with a bright hat and loud sub, country dry and centred, ska roomy. Route via `AdvancedFields` + `skafinity.config.json` so it retunes without a rebuild. |
| 22 | Per-section tempo | Tempo per section rather than per song (slow intro, faster outro). Buffer sizing already sums per-part, and `Timing` already walks a per-tick sample delta — this is varying that delta as the accumulator is built, plus sizing the buffer off the finished accumulator. |
| 18 | Ending ritard | Vary the accumulator's per-tick delta across the final bars. `RingOutTail` is a fixed 2.4 s, so the tail wants to grow with the ritard rather than stay constant. |
| 14 | Non-4/4 | Reachable now: `Timing` already carries `BeatsPerBar` and a `BeatGrouping` array (nothing reads the grouping yet), and `ComposePlan` picks the meter in one place. 6/8 and 12/8 first (12/8 is the natural home of a one-drop shuffle; a country bass lesson in 6/8 turned up in the research). Changing meter *within* a song needs more thinking — section maps would carry it. |
| 6 | `make dist` — single-file bundle | The one non-musical thing still deferred, and the Makefile currently exits 1 with a note. A true self-contained `.html` needs the whole .NET runtime + assemblies base64-inlined (multi-MB). Until it exists the toy is only distributable as a served directory, which is fine for the hosted site but not for "hand someone a file". |
